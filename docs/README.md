# Koyala — Documentation Index

Koyala is an AI-assisted mental health companion: private, culturally aware, evidence-based emotional support in English and Indian languages, with built-in crisis detection and a bridge to human care.

Documents are written in dependency order — each builds on the ones before it.

| # | Document | Question it answers | Primary owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | [MRD — Market Requirements](01-MRD-Market-Requirements.md) | Is there a market need, and what must we offer to win? | Product / Founders |
| 02 | [BRD — Business Requirements](02-BRD-Business-Requirements.md) | Why are we building this, and how does the business work? | Founders |
| 03 | [PRD — Product Requirements](03-PRD-Product-Requirements.md) | What exactly are we building, for whom, in what order? | Product |
| 04 | [TDD — Technical Design](04-TDD-Technical-Design.md) | How is the system built? | Engineering |
| 05 | [AI Model Specification](05-AI-Model-Specification.md) | How do the AI components behave, get trained and get evaluated? | AI/ML + Clinical |
| 06 | [Clinical Safety & Crisis Protocol](06-Clinical-Safety-Protocol.md) | How do we keep users safe and respond to crises? | Clinical Safety Officer |
| 07 | [Privacy, Security & Compliance](07-Privacy-Security-Compliance.md) | What laws apply and how do we protect data? | DPO / Legal / Security |

## Status

All documents are **v0.1 drafts**. Items marked **[verify]** (statistics, helpline numbers, legal timelines) must be confirmed against primary sources and by qualified legal/clinical reviewers before use. Doc 06 requires Clinical Advisory Board sign-off before any user exposure.

## Requirement ID conventions

- `MR-xx` market requirement (MRD) · `BR-xx` business requirement (BRD)
- `FR-<AREA>-xx` functional and `NFR-xx` non-functional requirement (PRD)
- `C#` AI component, `R#`/`M#` model behaviour rules (AI Model Spec)
