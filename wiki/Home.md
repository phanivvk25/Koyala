# Koyala

**An AI-assisted mental health companion** — private, culturally aware, evidence-based emotional support in English and Indian languages, with built-in crisis detection and a bridge to human care.

> **Koyala is not an emergency service.** In India, call Tele-MANAS **14416** or emergency **112**.

## Status

Backend MVP. **Not ready for real users**: crisis content, helplines, exercises and the risk word list are placeholders awaiting clinical sign-off, and several integrations are stubs. See [[Roadmap]].
